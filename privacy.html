Privacy, plainly explained.

Draft policy — last updated November 22, 2026.

This is an early-stage website. At the moment, the site does not offer user accounts, comments, contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, analytics, or advertising. The local prototype does not intentionally collect personal information.

Third-party services

The current design loads fonts from Google Fonts. A visitor's browser may contact Google's servers to retrieve those font files. You can disable remote font loading by removing the font imports from the site's stylesheet and HTML.

Future changes

If analytics, a newsletter, embedded media, or advertising are added, this policy must be updated before those features launch to explain what data is processed, by whom, and how visitors can exercise their rights.

Contact

A public contact address has not been established yet. This page will be updated with a working contact method before the site is publicly launched.
